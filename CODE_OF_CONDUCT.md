# Code of Conduct

Karat contributors and maintainers commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good intent, and focus disagreements on technical decisions. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not accepted.

Report incidents privately to the project maintainers through the repository's private security/contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to repository spaces and public interactions representing Karat or XySpace.
