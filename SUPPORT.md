# Support and collaboration

- **Bugs:** open a bug report with a minimal reproduction, Karat version, and platform.
- **Features:** open a feature request; discuss major designs before implementation.
- **Questions and collaboration:** use GitHub Discussions.
- **Security:** do not open a public issue; follow `SECURITY.md`.

Karat is community-supported and provided without warranty. Sponsorship can help XySpace fund signing, release infrastructure, devices, and contributor work. The repository's GitHub Sponsors entry is preparatory and works only after the owner enables sponsorship.
